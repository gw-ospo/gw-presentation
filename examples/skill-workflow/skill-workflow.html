GW Brand Guidelines

communications.gwu.edu/brand-guidelines

GWU refreshed its brand.

New colors. New typefaces. New graphic elements drawn from campus architecture and the DC flag.

The guidelines live on three web pages — detailed, thoughtful, and written for designers, not for an AI.

The question was how to get that knowledge into Claude's hands, permanently and precisely.

1
Extract
Claude on Chrome
read the guidelines.

Using Claude's browser extension, three pages of brand documentation were visited and distilled — keeping only what a slide designer needs: colors, type, and graphic elements.

No copy-pasting. No manual transcription. Claude read the pages and produced structured Markdown files, ready to use.

GW Blue · GW Buff · Potomac · Navy Yard — plus five secondary accents with usage rules.
Typography
Three brand typefaces with roles defined. Google Fonts substitutes identified for free use online.
Gazzetta → Playfair Display  ·  Avenir Next → Nunito Sans  ·  Source Serif → Source Serif 4
Graphic Elements
Chevrons, Gate Crossbar, and star motifs — each with specific rules for orientation, color, and placement.
One pattern per slide. Single color only. Consistent throughout the deck.
